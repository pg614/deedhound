How to find Franklin County, Ohio sheriff sales

DeedHound doesn't list Franklin County sales yet. Here is where the official listings are and how the sales work.

Official links

  • Franklin County official sheriff sale page
  • Franklin County official online sheriff sale site (Realauction, run under contract with the Ohio Department of Administrative Services)

How sales work in Franklin County

  • Sheriff sales are held every Friday at 9:00 a.m. online at franklin.sheriffsaleauction.ohio.gov (in person at the Franklin County Courthouse, 373 S. High St., only when a court orders it). No sales on federal holidays.
  • Properties are advertised for three consecutive weeks in the Tuesday Columbus Dispatch, which the Sheriff calls the legal source of information.
  • Bidders must register with Realauction and have the deposit ($2,000 / $5,000 / $10,000, set by law) in place: wires by 5 p.m. two business days before, ACH by 4 p.m. seven days before the Friday auction.
  • Properties open for proxy bidding at least seven days before the sale.
  • Real Estate Sales unit: (614) 525-4231. Clerk of Courts Civil Division (case files): 345 S. High St., (614) 525-3621.

Source: https://sheriff.franklincountyohio.gov/Services/Real-Estate-Sales

Frequently asked questions

Where can I see Franklin County sheriff sales?

On the state's official sheriff sale website for the county (https://franklin.sheriffsaleauction.ohio.gov) and the county's official page (https://sheriff.franklincountyohio.gov/Services/Real-Estate-Sales).

What is the minimum bid at an Ohio sheriff sale?

For most foreclosure sales the property cannot sell for less than two-thirds of its court-ordered appraised value (Ohio Revised Code 2329.20). If it does not sell, it is typically offered again at a second sale with no minimum, as published for each property. Source: https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-revised-code/section-2329.20

How much deposit do I need?

For residential property, Ohio law sets the deposit by appraised value: $2,000 if appraised at $10,000 or less, $5,000 if over $10,000 up to $200,000, and $10,000 if over $200,000 (Ohio Revised Code 2329.211). The plaintiff/judgment creditor is not required to make a deposit. Source: https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-revised-code/section-2329.211

Can the owner still stop the sale?

An Ohio owner can redeem the property by paying the judgment, interest and costs any time before the court confirms the sale (Ohio Revised Code 2329.33). Sales are also often cancelled or postponed, so always re-check the official list. Source: https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-revised-code/section-2329.33
