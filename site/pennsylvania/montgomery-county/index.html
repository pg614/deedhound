How do Philadelphia and Montgomery County sheriff sales work?

Both counties hold sheriff sales online at Bid4Assets. You register free, send a deposit before the auction ($10,000 for mortgage foreclosure sales; $1,500 for Philadelphia tax sales, plus a $35 fee), and bid online. Philadelphia wants 10% plus a buyer's premium the next business day and the balance within 15 days; Montgomery wants the full balance within 12 calendar days. Source: https://phillysheriff.com/frequently-asked-questions-about-online-sheriff-sales/

How do Lehigh County sheriff sales work?

Lehigh County holds sales in person at 10:00 AM at the courthouse, 455 West Hamilton Street, Allentown. The winner pays 10% right away by certified or cashier's check, plus poundage and transfer taxes; the balance is due within 30 days or the deposit can be forfeited. The lender can adjourn a sale up to 130 days. Source: https://www.lehighcounty.org/Departments/Sheriffs-Office/Sheriff-Sale

Do I get clear title at a Pennsylvania sheriff sale?

Not guaranteed. Properties are sold as-is, and the sheriffs say bidders are responsible for searching liens and mortgages that may not be paid off from the sale (the 'Schedule of Distribution'). Get a title search before you bid. Source: https://phillysheriff.com/real-estate/conditions-of-sale-mortgage/
