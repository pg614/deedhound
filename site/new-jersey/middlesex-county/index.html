Can a New Jersey sheriff sale be postponed?

Yes. New Jersey law lets the defendant (owner) request up to two adjournments of a sheriff sale, and the plaintiff or court can postpone it further (N.J.S.A. 2A:17-36). That is why many listings show several postponements. Source: https://lis.njleg.state.nj.us/

Can the owner redeem after the sale?

Under the New Jersey Court Rules (R. 4:65-5) there is a 10-day period after the sale for objections, and the owner may redeem during that time before the deed is delivered. Source: https://www.njcourts.gov/attorneys/rules-of-court
