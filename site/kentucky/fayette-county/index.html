Is there a minimum bid at a Kentucky master commissioner sale?

Kentucky law does not set a fixed minimum bid. But if a court-ordered sale brings less than two-thirds (2/3) of the court appraisal, the former owner can buy the property back (redeem it) within six months of the sale by repaying the price plus 10% a year interest and certain costs (KRS 426.530). Buyers who want to avoid that risk bid at least 2/3 of the appraisal. Source: https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/law/statutes/statute.aspx?id=45200

How do I pay if I win?

The Jefferson and Fayette commissioners' published terms: pay cash, or pay a 10% deposit at the sale and post a bond for the balance, which is due within 30 days with interest (KRS 426.705 bond). Check each sale's official notice. Source: https://faycom.info/upcoming/

Who runs the sale?

In Kentucky, foreclosure sales ordered by the circuit court are held by the court's Master Commissioner, not the sheriff. Jefferson County sales are held at the Hall of Justice in Louisville; Fayette County sales at 120 North Limestone in Lexington unless held online. Source: https://www.jeffcomm.org/
